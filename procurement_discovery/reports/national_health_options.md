## Opioid-settlement funds (nationwide, ~18-year payouts)

The archetype of a purchasable, buildable, no-PHI product is Delaware County, PA, which used settlement funds for a **cloud "Opioid Resource Surveillance" system**:

- treatment and detox services directory
- bed and program availability
- public search
- dashboard

That RFP ran in 2024. Similar needs recur across counties, and some counties allow businesses to apply (e.g. Steele County MN, which opens fall 2026). Incumbents (findhelp, Bamboo OpenBeds, Behavioral Health Link) mainly serve larger buyers.
